Commercial Door Types

Commercial Garage Doors We Service in the Five Towns

Commercial garage doors are built to different specifications than residential doors — heavier springs, higher-gauge cables, industrial operators, and cycle ratings measured in tens of thousands of operations rather than thousands. We service the full range of commercial door types found in Five Towns business properties.

Sectional Commercial Doors

Commercial sectional doors — the standard door type for most Five Towns warehouse, storage, and light industrial properties — operate on the same general principle as residential sectional doors but with heavier construction. Commercial torsion springs are wound to higher torque, cables are heavier gauge, and the tracks are stronger to handle the additional door weight.

Spring failure is the most common commercial door emergency we handle in the Five Towns. We carry commercial spring inventory in a range of cycle ratings — standard cycle, high-cycle, and commercial high-cycle for high-traffic applications.

Spring, cable & track repair

Rolling Steel Doors

Rolling steel doors — also called coiling doors or roll-up doors — are common on commercial storefronts, self-storage facilities, and loading docks throughout the Five Towns commercial districts. They coil into a drum above the opening rather than tracking along the ceiling, making them space-efficient for tight commercial spaces.

Rolling steel door failures typically involve broken torsion springs on the coil drum, worn barrel bearings, or failed motor operators. We repair and replace all components on rolling steel doors and service the commercial operators that automate them.

Fire-Rated Doors

Fire-rated garage doors are required by Nassau County building code in commercial occupancies where a rated wall assembly separates the garage from other building areas. They are designed to close automatically upon activation of a fusible link or fire alarm signal, providing a rated barrier against fire spread.

Fire-rated door maintenance and inspection is not optional — it's a code requirement. We inspect, test, lubricate, and service fire-rated commercial doors throughout the Five Towns and provide documentation for compliance records.

Commercial Operators & Controls

Commercial door operators — the motorized drive units for sectional and rolling steel doors — are built to handle much higher cycle counts and heavier loads than residential openers. We service commercial operators from all major manufacturers.

Common commercial operator failures include worn drive gears, failed limit switches, damaged control boards, and broken emergency releases. We diagnose and repair commercial operators same day throughout the Five Towns, and install replacement units when repair is not cost-effective. For residential-style openers on smaller commercial doors, see our garage door opener service.

Do commercial garage door springs need to be replaced more often than residential?

Commercial springs are rated for higher cycle counts than residential springs — typically 50,000 to 100,000 cycles versus 10,000 to 25,000 for standard residential springs. However, high-traffic commercial doors — auto shops, loading docks, storage facilities — may cycle the door 20 to 50 times per day, which adds up to 7,000 to 18,000 cycles per year.
