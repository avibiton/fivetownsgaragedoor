Why Replace Your Garage Door

When a Repair Isn't Enough — What Five Towns Homeowners Should Know

Most garage door problems in the Five Towns are repaired, not replaced — and we'll always tell you honestly if repair is the better option. But there are situations where a new door is the right call, and recognizing them early saves money in the long run.

When multiple panels are cracked, rusted through, or badly dented, panel-by-panel replacement quickly approaches the cost of a new door — while leaving you with mismatched sections and an aging frame. When the door frame itself is bent or warped, no repair will restore smooth, quiet operation. When an older door lacks insulation and the garage is attached to the house, upgrading to an insulated door can meaningfully reduce heating and cooling costs through the shared wall.

The Five Towns communities — particularly the South Shore properties in Lawrence, Cedarhurst, and Inwood — deal with accelerated corrosion from salt air. Steel doors less than 0.5 miles from the water often show rust within 8–10 years. Upgrading to galvanized steel or aluminum construction adds significant lifespan at a modest cost difference.

We provide a free in-home estimate with no obligation. During the estimate, we measure your opening, assess the condition of the existing hardware and framing, and explain every available option at every price point — so you can make the right decision for your home and your budget.

Curb Appeal

The garage door is one of the most visible elements of a home's exterior — often covering 30% or more of the front facade. In the Five Towns, a door that matches the architectural style of the house commands attention from buyers and neighbors alike. A carriage house door on a colonial, or a clean flush panel on a contemporary renovation, makes a visible difference.

{{icon:thermo}}

Energy Efficiency

An uninsulated garage door in an attached garage creates a thermal bridge between the conditioned interior of the house and the outside. Where the garage shares a wall with the kitchen or a bedroom, a door with R-12 polyurethane foam insulation can make a noticeable difference in the temperature of adjacent rooms — and on the heating bill through a Nassau County winter.

{{icon:volume}}

Noise Reduction

Triple-layer insulated steel doors are significantly quieter than single-layer doors — both in the door itself flexing as it opens and closes, and in sound transmission from outside. For homes in Hewlett and Woodmere where the garage is directly under a bedroom, the difference is immediately apparent.

Insulation, R-Value & Windows

Choosing Insulation and Window Options for Your New Door

R-value measures how well a door resists heat flow — the higher the number, the better it insulates. For an attached garage, it's one of the most important choices you'll make.

The majority of Five Towns homes have attached garages, and insulation matters significantly there — an uninsulated door is essentially a large thermal opening in the building envelope. For attached garages we recommend a minimum of R-12 polyurethane insulation. Polyurethane foam also bonds the steel layers together, which stiffens the door and is part of why insulated doors run quieter. For detached garages where the space isn't conditioned, a single-layer door may be the more economical choice.

The Installation Process

What to Expect When We Install Your New Door

  1. Free In-Home Measurement & Estimate

    We come to your Five Towns home, measure the opening precisely, assess the existing framing and hardware, and walk through every option at every price point. You receive a written estimate with no obligation before we leave. Most estimates take 30–45 minutes.

  2. Door Selection & Order

    Once you choose a door, we place the order with the manufacturer. Standard doors from our in-stock inventory are available for immediate installation. Custom color, size, or style orders typically take 2–4 weeks depending on the manufacturer and model.

  3. Removal of the Old Door

    On installation day, we remove the existing door — panels, tracks, springs, cables, and hardware — and dispose of it. We inspect the opening framing for rot, damage, or misalignment and address any issues found to ensure a clean, level installation.

  4. Installation, Balancing & Testing

    The new door is installed, the spring system is calibrated to the exact weight of the door, the opener (if applicable) is programmed and tested, and a full safety inspection is completed — auto-reverse test, sensor alignment, and balance check. Most installations are completed in 3–5 hours on a single day.

  5. Written Warranty & Walkthrough

    Before we leave, we walk you through the operation of the new door, show you any new features (battery backup, MyQ app, keypad), and provide your written warranty documentation. All installation debris and the old door are removed from the property.

Five Towns Considerations

What Makes Five Towns Installations Different

Installing a garage door in the Five Towns isn't the same as installing one inland. The communities closest to the water — Lawrence, Inwood, and parts of Cedarhurst — deal with salt air corrosion that accelerates hardware wear and surface rust on standard steel doors. We address this proactively.

{{icon:wave}}Coastal & Near-Water Properties

For properties close to Reynolds Channel, Jamaica Bay, or the South Shore barrier islands, we recommend galvanized steel door panels, stainless steel lifting cables, and nylon or stainless hardware throughout. The additional cost over standard hardware is modest — and the difference in lifespan is significant. We discuss this during the free estimate for every South Shore property, or call the South Shore line at (516) 612-9317.

Attached vs. Detached Garages

Most Five Towns homes have attached garages, where insulation matters significantly. For attached garages we recommend a minimum of R-12 polyurethane insulation. For detached garages where the space isn't conditioned, a single-layer door may be the more economical choice.
