Broken Torsion Spring

The torsion spring sits horizontally above the door opening and does most of the heavy lifting — literally. When it breaks, you'll typically hear a loud bang, and the door will become extremely heavy or won't open at all. Torsion spring failure is the most common garage door emergency we handle in the Five Towns. We carry springs in every standard size and replace them same day. Both springs are always inspected together — if one has broken, the other is typically near the end of its cycle life as well.

Do not attempt to operate the door until the spring is replaced.

From $295
{{icon:spring}}

Extension Spring Replacement

Extension springs run along the horizontal tracks on each side of the door and stretch to provide lifting force. They're common on older Five Towns homes with lower garage ceilings that can't accommodate a torsion spring setup. Extension springs are under significant tension even when the door is closed — never attempt to adjust or replace them yourself. We replace both springs simultaneously and always include safety cables to contain a spring if it ever snaps under load.

Extension springs must always be replaced in pairs.

From $145
{{icon:cable}}

Snapped & Frayed Cable Replacement

Lifting cables run from the bottom corners of the door up and around the drum pulleys at the top. When a cable frays, kinks, or snaps, the door drops unevenly and becomes impossible to operate safely. Salt air in the South Shore communities of Cedarhurst, Lawrence, and Inwood accelerates cable corrosion — we recommend galvanized or stainless steel cables for properties within a mile of the water. We always replace both cables together, regardless of which one failed, so an imbalance doesn't return immediately.

From $125
{{icon:gear}}

Roller & Track Service

Metal rollers are the most common source of grinding and scraping noise in Five Towns garages. Over time the steel ball bearings wear out, the roller stem bends, or the nylon cracks and the roller begins dragging in the track. Upgrading to sealed nylon ball-bearing rollers dramatically reduces noise and wear on the tracks themselves. Bent or misaligned tracks are straightened and re-secured to the wall — a bent track left unaddressed will eventually cause the door to come off track completely and create an emergency.

From $35 per roller

Off-Track Door Repair

When a door comes off its track — usually caused by a broken cable, a vehicle collision, or an obstruction in the track — it must be treated as an emergency. The door becomes structurally unstable and can fall at any moment. Do not try to force it back into position yourself. We realign the door, inspect the track, cables, and rollers for damage, and test the full operation before leaving. Off-track repairs are handled same day throughout Cedarhurst, Hewlett, Lawrence, Woodmere, and Inwood.

Emergency service — do not operate an off-track door.

Hinge, Panel & Hardware

Hinges are the joints between each panel of a sectional garage door. When a hinge cracks or bends — common on older steel doors in the Five Towns — the section alignment changes and the door begins to bind in the tracks. Cracked or dented panels are replaced individually when the door is structurally intact; when multiple panels are damaged, a full door replacement may be more economical. We carry standard hinge sets on every truck and complete most hardware repairs the same visit.

From $80

Noisy Garage Doors

Grinding usually means worn metal rollers running in dry tracks, loose hardware vibrating against the door, or a failing opener gear. A door that has grown steadily louder is an early warning, not just an annoyance — worn rollers eventually seize, and loose hardware eventually fails. We find the actual source, tighten and lubricate the hardware, and stock nylon roller upgrade kits on every truck.

Opener Problems

An opener that hums but won't move the door, a door that reverses before it closes, or blinking sensor lights all point to the opener system. Reversal is usually misaligned photo-eye safety sensors or a force setting calibrated too sensitively. A slow door can also mean a weak spring forcing the motor to compensate. We repair LiftMaster openers, Genie openers, and other major brands.

When to Call

Warning Signs Five Towns Homeowners Should Never Ignore

Most major garage door failures in the Five Towns don't happen without warning. These are the signs that something is wrong — and that waiting will make it worse and more expensive.

{{icon:bolt}}

Loud Bang From the Garage

Almost always a torsion spring breaking under load. Stop using the door immediately and call for same-day repair.

{{icon:alert}}

Door Opens Unevenly

One side rises faster than the other — a classic sign of a broken or stretched cable on one side. Do not continue to operate.

{{icon:volume}}

Grinding or Scraping Noise

Metal rollers wearing against dry tracks. Left unaddressed, the rollers eventually seize and the door comes off track.

{{icon:clock}}

Door Moves Slower Than Usual

A broken or weakened spring is forcing the opener motor to compensate. This burns out the motor if not corrected.

{{icon:opener}}

Door Reverses Before Closing

Misaligned safety sensors or a force setting that's too sensitive. Both are adjusted during a same-day service call.

{{icon:cable}}

Visible Cable Fraying

A frayed cable can snap at any time. Both cables should be replaced immediately — before the door drops unexpectedly.

My garage door cable snapped — can I still use the door?

No. A door with a broken cable is structurally unbalanced and can fall suddenly on the unsupported side. Do not operate the opener or try to lift the door manually. Call (516) 490-0931 for same-day cable repair throughout the Five Towns. We carry galvanized and stainless steel cables on every truck for properties near the water.

Can you repair a garage door that came off its track?

Yes — off-track doors are one of the most common emergency calls we receive in the Five Towns. Do not force the door. Call (516) 490-0931. We realign the door, inspect for damage, and test the full system before leaving.

Why does my garage door make a grinding noise?

Grinding is most often worn metal rollers running dry in the tracks. As the steel ball bearings wear out, the roller drags instead of rolls — creating friction and noise. Left unaddressed, the rollers eventually seize completely and the door comes off track. Loose hardware vibrating against the door or a failing opener gear can also cause grinding. All three are repaired same day, and we stock sealed nylon ball-bearing roller upgrade kits on every truck. Call (516) 490-0931.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

We'll give you an honest recommendation during the free inspection. As a rule: if the door structure is sound and only the hardware or springs have failed, repair is almost always the better value. If multiple panels are cracked, the door frame is bent, or the hardware has completely worn through after years of service, a replacement door may save you money over the next few years in repeated repairs.

My door reverses before it closes — what's causing it?

Usually one of two things: misaligned photo-eye safety sensors at the bottom of the door opening, or an opener force setting that's calibrated too sensitively. Both are adjusted during a service call. Occasionally a physical obstruction in the track or a door that's out of balance can also trigger the reversal. We diagnose and fix the actual cause — not just reset the opener. Call (516) 490-0931.

Do you repair garage doors on weekends in the Five Towns?

Yes — we're available evenings, weekends, and holidays throughout the Five Towns at the same rate as weekday service. Garage door failures don't wait for Monday morning, and neither do we. Call (516) 490-0931 or the South Shore line at (516) 612-9317 any time.
