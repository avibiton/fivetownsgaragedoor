LiftMaster Service

Precision LiftMaster Diagnostics & Repair

LiftMaster is the industry standard for residential and commercial garage door operators across the Five Towns. Known for robust Security+ 2.0 radio encryption and smooth DC soft start/stop motors, these systems provide exceptional longevity when properly matched to door weight.

When an opener fails, we do not guess. If your motor hums helplessly without turning, the internal nylon helical drive gear (Part #41A2817) has typically sheared to protect the motor from burning out against an unbalanced door. We replace the gear and sprocket assembly, apply low-temperature synthetic grease, and recalibrate door balance in one visit.

For homes with coastal storm outages or attached garages adjacent to bedrooms, we install factory-fresh DC belt drive and wall-mount jackshaft systems with full manufacturer warranties.

Common LiftMaster Problems

LiftMaster Problems We Diagnose & Repair

Describe the symptom when you call — most LiftMaster failures point to a specific part, and we bring it on the truck.

{{icon:gear}}

Motor Hums, Door Doesn't Move

A stripped sacrificial white nylon drive gear (Part #41A2817) inside the powerhead. We rebuild the gear and sprocket assembly, re-grease, and recalibrate door balance in one visit.

$130 – $149
{{icon:bolt}}

Remotes & Wall Button Stopped Working

South Shore electrical storms and coastal power grid surges frequently damage Security+ 2.0 receiver logic boards. We carry OEM circuit boards and reprogram all remotes on-site.

$165
{{icon:shield}}

Door Reverses or Won't Close

Usually misaligned or failed safety photo-eyes (41A5034) at the bottom of the door opening. We realign or replace the sensors and test auto-reverse before we leave.

$75 – $150
{{icon:battery}}

Battery Backup No Longer Holding

Battery-backup LiftMaster openers use a standby 12V 4.5Ah battery that wears out with age. We replace it so the door keeps working through the next storm outage.

$115
{{icon:wifi}}

Lost Remote or New Keypad

We sync 893MAX remotes and 878MAX wireless keypads to your LiftMaster opener, and program remotes for added household vehicles.

$45 – $85
{{icon:spring}}

Opener Straining on a Heavy Door

An opener is not designed to lift an unbalanced door — that strain is what shears drive gears. If the door is heavy or a spring is worn, we fix the springs and balance first so the opener repair lasts.

Repair or Replace

When to Repair Your LiftMaster — and When to Replace It

A failed gear or logic board doesn't mean you need a new opener. Age, repeat failures, and missing safety features do.

{{icon:wrench}}

Usually Worth Repairing

  • Motor hums but the door doesn't move — a gear kit rebuild, not a new opener
  • Remotes stopped responding after a storm or power surge — logic board replacement
  • Door reverses before closing — photo-eye realignment
  • Battery backup no longer holds a charge — standby battery replacement
Same-day garage door repair{{icon:arrow}}
{{icon:opener}}

Time to Replace

  • The opener is more than 15 years old
  • It has failed multiple times
  • It lacks photo-eye sensors and auto-reverse
  • You want battery backup, LED lighting, a camera, or MyQ smartphone control

Why does my LiftMaster opener hum but the door doesn't move?

A humming LiftMaster motor with stationary travel almost always indicates a stripped sacrificial white nylon drive gear (Part #41A2817) inside the powerhead chassis. We rebuild gear and sprocket assemblies same day throughout the Five Towns.

Why is the LiftMaster belt drive series best for Hewlett and Woodmere homes?

Hewlett and Woodmere colonials typically feature attached garages with bedrooms situated directly above. LiftMaster steel-reinforced rubber belt drives operate virtually silently, eliminating metallic vibration along ceiling joists.

What are the advantages of the LiftMaster 98022 wall-mount jackshaft?

The 98022 mounts flush to the wall beside the torsion spring bar, completely freeing ceiling space for overhead storage, car lifts, or finished cathedral rafters. It includes an automatic power deadbolt lock and battery backup.

Do you carry LiftMaster logic boards on your trucks?

Yes. South Shore electrical storms and coastal power grid surges frequently damage Security+ 2.0 receiver logic boards. We carry OEM circuit boards and reprogram all remotes on-site.

Can you add MyQ smartphone control to my existing LiftMaster?

Often, yes. We can install a MyQ hub on compatible existing LiftMaster openers, giving you remote open/close, left-open alerts, schedules, and Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant integration.
