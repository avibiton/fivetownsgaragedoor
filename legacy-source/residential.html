Seasonal Considerations

How Nassau County Seasons
Affect Five Towns Garage Doors

The Five Towns experience the full range of Nassau County weather — coastal nor'easters in winter, hot humid summers, and everything in between. Each season creates specific stresses on residential garage door systems that homeowners should be aware of.

❄️ Winter
Cold Weather & Spring Tension
Cold temperatures cause steel to contract — including garage door springs. A spring that was marginally functional in September may fail on the first genuinely cold morning in January. Grease and lubricant in tracks and hinges thickens in cold weather, increasing the load on springs and openers. Pre-winter lubrication with a product rated for cold temperatures is the best prevention. If the door feels heavier than usual in cold weather, call before the spring breaks.
🌿 Spring
Storm Season & Seal Inspection
Spring in the Five Towns brings nor'easters and heavy rain events. A failed bottom seal allows water into the garage during these storms — damaging stored items, flooring, and potentially the door's lower panels. Spring is the right time for an annual tune-up: the door has survived a winter, any cold-weather stress is visible, and the hardware is ready for lubrication and adjustment before the warm season begins.
☀️ Summer
Heat, Humidity & Salt Air
Summer humidity swells wood-composite door components and can cause metal panels to expand slightly — both can affect tracking. For South Shore Five Towns properties in Lawrence and Inwood, summer is when salt air corrosion is most active, as humidity accelerates the oxidation process on exposed steel cables and hardware. Summer lubrication and cable inspection is particularly valuable for waterfront and near-water properties.
🍂 Fall
Pre-Winter Preparation
Fall is the best time for a residential tune-up in the Five Towns. The door is about to face its hardest months — cold temperatures, ice, and storm stress. An October or November tune-up gives the system a clean bill of health before winter, replaces any hardware approaching end of life, and lubricates everything with cold-weather-appropriate products. It's the single most effective step Five Towns homeowners can take to avoid a January emergency call.
